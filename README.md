I’m a **Research Assistant** at DSV, Stockholm University. I recently finished my MSc in Data Science there, and before that I did a Bachelor’s in Computer Science & Engineering at BMSIT&M, Bangalore, followed by 4+ years of industry R&D building deep learning solutions.

These days I work mostly on **Agentic AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and prompt-driven AI workflows**, exploring how LLMs and Agents can be made more efficient, adaptive, and useful in real-world contexts. My expertise spans **natural language processing**, computer vision, and recommender systems. I’ve successfully **built and deployed several models** for text classification, sentiment analysis, non-intrusive load monitoring, and recommender pipelines, with a strong focus on reliability, evaluation, and practical deployment.

When I’m not working on projects, I enjoy **photography**, playing my **guitar**, and creative side-projects. I’m always excited to connect and collaborate on meaningful AI-driven work that creates real impact. I enjoy mentoring aspiring data scientists and have collaborated with them to strengthen their foundations in machine learning, deep learning, and applied research workflows.
